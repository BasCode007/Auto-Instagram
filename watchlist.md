## Launch-night early time zones

New Zealand unlocks first (00:00 NZDT = 11:00 UTC Nov 18), then Australia (00:00 AEDT = 13:00 UTC Nov 18), Japan (15:00 UTC Nov 18).
Search X, TikTok and Twitch for "GTA 6" + "New Zealand" / "NZ" / "Australia" in those windows to find the first real gameplay.
Ask before using a clip; credit always.
