## Story, people and places ([Only in Leonida](https://www.rockstargames.com/VI/only-in-leonida))

- Setting: Vice City, USA, and the state of Leonida. Vice City is "the darkest side of the sunniest place in America."
- Regions named on the official site: Vice City, Leonida Keys, Grassrivers, Port Gellhorn, Ambrosia, Mount Kalaga.
- **Jason Duval**: grew up around grifters and crooks; after a stint in the Army, ended up in the Keys working for local drug runners.
- **Lucia Caminos**: her father taught her to fight as soon as she could walk; fighting for her family landed her in the Leonida Penitentiary. Fresh out of prison.
- **Cal Hampton**: Jason's friend and a fellow associate of Brian's; snoops on Coast Guard comms.
- **Boobie Ike**: Vice City legend with a legit empire (real estate, a strip club, a recording studio); partner in Only Raw Records.
- **Dre'Quan Priest**: aspiring music mogul at Only Raw Records; signed the Real Dimez.
- **Real Dimez**: Bae-Luxe and Roxy; signed to Only Raw Records.
- **Raul Bautista**: seasoned bank robber looking for talent.
- **Brian Heder**: classic drug runner from the golden age of smuggling in the Keys; runs product through his boat yard with his third wife, Lori. Lets Jason live rent-free at one of his properties as long as he helps with local shakedowns.
- ESRB: Mature. Descriptors: Blood and Gore, Intense Violence, Mature Humor, Nudity, Strong Language, Strong Sexual Content, Use of Drugs and Alcohol. In-Game Purchases.
