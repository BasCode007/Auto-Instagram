# The production standard

Derived on 2026-11-12 from six reference reels the owner picked (shortcodes
DexQkKUzhox, DcFfATugXzz, DcY_8GzTPK7, DZOZhp3Jks0, DfhWMOsBY5t, DeUvpgIpFHU),
each watched scene by scene. What they share is the standard; what they do not
share is a style choice.

## What every reference does

| Element | The standard |
|---|---|
| Length | 12–40 s. Most are 12–15 s. Longer only when the script earns it. |
| Cut rate | **A new shot every 0.8–1.5 s.** Our first test cut every 4–7 s; that is the biggest gap. |
| Footage | Aspirational, cinematic, moody: private jets, supercars at night, Dubai/skyline, yachts, penthouses, rooftop pools, cash, watches, lone figure at golden hour. Real or AI, never "office stock". |
| Grade | High contrast, crushed blacks, warm highlights (gold/amber) against cool teal/blue. Slight desaturation. |
| Captions | **Huge, centred, 1–3 words per beat**, popping in on the spoken word. Bold extended sans (Montserrat Black / The Bold Font) as the base; one accent face (editorial serif or script) in gold for the emphasised word. White with soft shadow. Mid-frame, not low. |
| Voice | Deep, calm, authoritative male narration at a measured pace, or a real speaker clip. One reel used a calm neutral female TTS voice. Never upbeat "creator" energy. |
| Music | Atmospheric cinematic pad / phonk / lo-fi with sub pulse, mixed only 10–14 dB under the voice (louder than our test). Text-only reels run music at full volume. |
| Hook | A counter-intuitive command or claim in the first 2 s: "Be selfish for the next two years", "Your parents are preparing you for the wrong life", "Don't waste it on a small life". |
| Branding | None or a tiny centred watermark. No top label. |
| CTA | None. Retention and shares come from the idea, not a "follow for more". |

## Two formats to produce

1. **Narrated montage** (the main format): 20–35 s script, deep voice,
   a cut every ~1.2 s, 1–2 word captions synced to speech, music at -12 dB.
2. **Text-only montage**: 10–15 s, one static line of elegant serif text
   centred ("We are born for this"), music at full volume, cuts on the beat.

## Script rules (replaces the earlier 75–115 word guidance)

- 45–80 words. Open with the counter-intuitive line; no greeting, no "today".
- Short declarative sentences; each sentence is a shot.
- End on the strongest line, not on a CTA.

## Sources at this standard

- Footage: AI video (ComfyUI, see `docs/COMFYUI.md`) for signature shots;
  stock (vidIQ b-roll / Pexels HD renditions) for fills. Never movie clips.
- Voice: ElevenLabs via vidIQ until the local replacement is in place.
- Music: one generated track per mood, reused.
- Composite: Picsart scene (`pipeline_v2/`).
